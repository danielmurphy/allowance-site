Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

Allowance ("the App") helps families track kids' allowance money. You can use it on one device without an account, and nothing you enter leaves that device. If you turn on family sharing, your family's data is stored on our servers so the grown-ups and kids you invite can see it. We don't sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Using Allowance Without an Account

Without an account, everything you enter is stored only on your device. That includes kids' names and colors, account balances (Spend, Save, Share), and transactions (amounts, notes, dates). Lightweight preferences, such as whether you've finished setup, are stored on your device too.

Accounts

Family sharing requires signing in with Apple or Google. We receive and store:

  • Your name and email address (Sign in with Apple lets you hide your email)
  • A user ID created by our sign-in provider, Firebase Authentication

We use your name so the other people in your family know who you are. The other grown-ups in your family can see your name and email. Your name is also shown to kids' devices in your family and to anyone you give a family code to, so they know who invited them.

Family Sharing

When you sign in, your family's data is copied to our servers and kept in sync across your family's devices:

  • Family name
  • Kids' names, colors and order
  • Account balances and transactions, including any notes you write
  • Allowance amounts and when they were last paid
  • Who's in the family, and when they joined

Only grown-ups in your family can see or change this data. Family codes for inviting someone can only be used once and are deleted when used or cancelled.

Kids' Devices

A grown-up can set up a kid's device with a code. The kid's device doesn't need an account, email, or any personal information. It gets a random ID that lets it read that one kid's balances and transactions. It can't change anything or see other kids. Any grown-up in the family can turn off access at any time.

Subscriptions

Family sharing is a subscription purchased through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details or Apple ID. To unlock sharing for your family, we store the subscription's transaction ID, its status and renewal date, and which family member bought it. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Where Data Is Stored

Shared data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) in the United States. It's encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is limited by security rules on our servers.

Backup & Export

The App can export your data as a file saved wherever you choose, such as the Files app or iCloud Drive. We don't have access to those files. You're responsible for keeping them secure.

Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to count anonymous events about how it's used, such as finishing setup, adding a kid or recording a transaction, so we can improve it. An event can include general details, like a count or the kind of transaction or account.

These events never include names, amounts, notes or your account. Firebase Analytics may also collect standard device information (model, OS version and app version) and an approximate location, such as your country, region or city, worked out from your internet connection rather than GPS. See Google's Firebase privacy documentation.

Crash Reporting

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google) to send a report when it crashes. Reports include the device model, OS version, app version, and technical details about the crash. They don't include names, balances or transactions.

No Ads or Tracking

Allowance has no ads, doesn't use advertising identifiers, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. Analytics data isn't used for advertising.

Children's Privacy

Allowance is managed by grown-ups. Kids don't create accounts or provide personal information. A kid's name and money records are entered by a grown-up, who controls them and can change or delete them at any time. A kid's device only reads what a grown-up has shared with it, and doesn't send analytics. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

Deleting Your Data

  • On your device: delete kids or reset data in the App, or delete the App.
  • Leaving a family: your name and email are removed from that family. Your device keeps a copy of the family's data.
  • Deleting your account: in the App, go to Settings → Family sharing → Delete account. This deletes your account and removes you from your family. If other grown-ups remain, the family stays with them. If you're the last grown-up, it also deletes the family's shared data, its codes, and access for its kids' devices. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the App's Sign in with Apple access. Your device keeps a copy of the data.
  • Kids' devices: any grown-up can turn off a kid's device from the App, which deletes its access. Deleting a kid from a shared family also deletes access for that kid's device.

Deleted data is removed from our servers right away and from our backups within 100 days. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing us.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and subscriptions
  • Google: Google Sign-In, and Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics)

These services are subject to Apple's privacy policy and Google's Firebase privacy policy.

Changes to This Policy
