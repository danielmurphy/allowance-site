Help

Answers to common questions about Allowance.

Do I need an account to use Allowance?

No. Allowance works fully on one device, for free, with no account. You only sign in if you want to share with your family.

How do I share with another grown-up?

Family sharing lets another grown-up, like a co-parent or grandparent, see and change everything. In Allowance, go to Settings → Family sharing → Invite a grown-up. You get a 6-character code, a QR code and a link, each good for one use. Send one to them. They open it on their device, or tap "Scan or type a code" when they first open Allowance, and sign in with Apple or Google.

How do I set up my kid's device?

Kids can see their own balances and history on their own iPhone or iPad. A grown-up sets it up with a kid code. In Allowance, go to Settings → Family sharing → Kids' devices, choose the kid and tap Set up. On the kid's device, install Allowance, tap Get started → Scan or type a code, and scan or enter the code. The kid's device is read-only. Any grown-up can turn it off anytime.

Does my kid need an account?

No. Kids don't need an account or an email address. The kid code is all it takes.

What does family sharing cost? How do I cancel?

Family sharing is $9.99 a year through Apple, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers. One plan covers the whole family. To cancel, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device, choose Allowance and cancel. Apple handles billing and renewals.

What happens if I stop paying?

Sharing pauses. Everyone keeps a copy of the data on their own device, so nothing is lost. You can still use Allowance on one device, for free.

How do I delete my account?

In Allowance, go to Settings → Family sharing → Delete account. If you're the last grown-up, the family's shared data is deleted too. Your device keeps a copy. See the privacy policy for what gets deleted.
